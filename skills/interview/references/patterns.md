# Interview — the craft

Practical techniques for building prep briefs, conducting live drills, executing realistic mocks, and processing post-interview debriefs.

## The brief — construction protocol

1. **Intake & Reuse:** Pull from `jd-analysis/`, `company/`, `profile.md`, and `storybank.md`. Check `question-bank.md` for banked real questions first.
2. **Format Discovery:** Identify platform (video, phone, onsite) and round format (behavioral screen, technical screen, system design, case, panel, bar-raiser).
3. **Company & Interviewer Intelligence:** Sourced reading of company culture and interviewer public professional profiles.
4. **Concern & Counter Formulation:**
   - *Dealbreaker:* Fundamental candidacy threats.
   - *Significant:* Will arise; requires full 3-part counter (direct framing, probe handling, challenge defense).
   - *Minor:* Probe-only; 1-sentence counter.
5. **Story Mapping:** Map predicted questions to confirmed `storybank.md` stories (`Strong Fit`, `Workable`, `Stretch`, `Gap`). Gaps use named handling patterns (Adjacent Bridge, Reframe/Growth, Hypothetical with Self-Awareness).
6. **Reverse Questions (5):** Tailor 5 questions with purpose, stage fit, reversal triggers, and candidate counters.
7. **Day-of Cheat Sheet:** 60s pitch reel, 3×3 core anchors, focus cue, warm-up prompt, and stated comp constraints.

## The gated practice ladder

| Stage | Drill | Gate to advance | Prereq |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ladder (same story at 30s/60s/90s/3min) | Structure ≥3 on 3 consecutive rounds | — |
| 2 | Pushback (challenging assertions) | Credibility ≥3 under pressure | 1 |
| 3 | Pivot (question redirection) | Relevance ≥3 when redirected | 2 |
| 4 | Gap ("I don't have an example") | Credibility ≥3 with honest gap handling | 2 |
| 5 | Role (specialist scrutiny) | Substance ≥3 under scrutiny | 1–3 |
| 6 | Panel (multiple personas) | All dimensions ≥3 | 1–4 |
| 7 | Stress (maximum pressure) | All dimensions ≥3 | 1–5 |
| 8 (opt) | Technical communication | Structure+Substance ≥3 | 1–3 |

## Round protocol

- **Warmup:** 1 unscored warmup question.
- **Scoring:** Coach scores across 5 dimensions *before* asking candidate's read.
- **Delta feedback:** Compare coach score with candidate self-rating to surface calibration gaps.
- **Interviewer's Read:** Quote candidate's words highlighting highest-teaching-value moments.
- **Assigned Change:** Exactly ONE concrete adjustment for the subsequent round.

## Real interview debrief protocol

- Run immediately post-interview.
- **4 Simultaneous Writes:**
  1. Capture session to `practice/<date>-real-<company>.md`.
  2. Append round record to `practice-log.md`.
  3. Bank questions asked to `question-bank.md` (keep approximate recall marked as vague).
  4. Increment story use counts in `storybank.md`.
- Conceded points under pushback are struck immediately in `base-resume.md § Claim rules`.
- Stated comp figures recorded in `negotiation/<company>.md`.

## Proposing a new pattern

If a session shows something works, say so with evidence. A pattern is never self-adopted, and a proposed one lands in the candidate's workspace, not in this file.
