# The gate grammar — one shape for every human gate

*(A shared contract, not coach's own technique: apply and outreach render every gate by this file, and PRINCIPLES.md §7 names it. Coach hosts it because the gates are coaching moments. It moves only with every consumer rewired in the same commit.)* **Host-neutral, shared verbatim across products** (this plugin and 10xjobs v4 — design-substrate-convergence.md). A "gate" is any action that is identity-bearing (a claim about the candidate, a message as them), relationship-bearing (any send), or irreversible (submit, accept, decline). The agent prepares; the human fires. Every gate renders the SAME way so the candidate always knows when they are at one.

## The shape (three parts, in order)

1. **The artifact** — the complete thing to be fired (the full draft message, the filled form summary, the exact number), presented for review with an open/edit path. Never a description of it; the thing itself.
2. **The gate line** — one explicit sentence naming what firing means and who fires:
   - Sends: "⚠ This sends as YOU. I never send — copy it, send it yourself, then tell me so I log it."
   - Submits: "Ready to submit. Say the word and I click — nothing is sent until you do."
   - Numbers/offers: "This number goes to the employer the moment you say it — confirm it's the one."
   - Spends: "This spends $<amount> — confirm before I commit it."
3. **The report-back** — after the human fires (or declines), the agent logs the outcome (date, follow-up cadence, pipeline stage) and confirms in one line what was recorded.

## Rules

- A gate NEVER fires from a button, a card tap, or a scheduled run — taps and prefills may *stage* the ask; only the candidate's explicit go in conversation fires it.
- One gate per confirm — never batch "send all three?" unless the candidate explicitly opted into a batch, and even then list each item.
- Declining is a full outcome: record it, don't re-ask the same gate unprompted.
- The gate line is not boilerplate to compress away — it IS the product's safety UX. Keep it even when the candidate is in a hurry; shorten the artifact framing instead.
