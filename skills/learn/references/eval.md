# Learn — how to judge the work, and the status standard

`SKILL.md` names the destination; this file is how to tell it is
reached, and who checks.

## Who checks what

- **Structure and counts → `scripts/check_knowledge.py`** (the Map's
  scope and status grammar — FAIL on a malformed scope or a bad status)
  and `../../profile/scripts/check_files.py` (the manifest). Both at
  session close.
- **Language against written rules → no checker-subagent.** Courses and
  assessments are the coach's voice.
- **Everything semantic → you, at the moment**: whether a source is
  canon or a blog, whether a quiz answer covered a dimension, whether
  a second sighting is real.

## The destination, judged

- **Every Map row has a scope the script accepts, a source with its
  sighting count, and a depth tied to the band.** Track scope only on
  two sightings or the lane definition — never on one JD's enthusiasm
  (t9-scope-discipline).
- **Every assessment left two writes the same turn**: the log row and
  the Map row's Assessed cell.
- **No credential was issued** — `credible` in its exact meaning, date shown, the caveat stated — and the candidate was told what they CAN honestly say (t9-never-certify).
- **Every course cites tiered sources**, carries its research date and
  honesty header, and exists even when research was thin — the
  sourceable canon built, the unknowns asked (t9-uncited-canon).
- **Every status or scope change was named in the reply** — a promotion
  changes what coach's groundwork sensing counts.

## The status standard

The four statuses and their lifecycle are declared in `schema.md § Status lifecycle`; what is judged here is that a status moved only on logged evidence and that `credible` was used in its exact meaning.

## Quiz scoring

Per checklist dimension: covered / partial / missed, at the stated
depth — plus the candidate's self-rating; the gap between the two is
coaching data. A strong answer is said to be strong, with evidence —
honesty runs both ways.
