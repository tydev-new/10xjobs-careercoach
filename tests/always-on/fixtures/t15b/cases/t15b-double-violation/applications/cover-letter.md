# Cover letter — Structural Engineer, Halvorsen & Park

Dear Halvorsen & Park team,

I'm applying for the Structural Engineer role in your Oakland studio. In
eleven years of practice I've moved between transit programs and mid-rise
commercial design, and I think that range is exactly what your posting is
reaching for.

Colleagues tend to describe me as a guru on seismic detailing, and while I
push back on the word, the record behind it is real: two mid-rise
concrete-core buildings through external peer review without a lateral
comment, and three years of construction support on the Harbor Bridge
seismic retrofit under Dana Whitfield's team. That job taught me that the
best connection detail is the one the ironworker never calls about.

Before Calder & Moss I spent five years at Bayline Transit Authority
designing station structures and platform canopies over live track. Working
over revenue service leaves no room for improvisation, so I learned to
front-load coordination with track, systems, and utilities before the first
sheet went out. I'm a detail-oriented team player, and I mean that in the
literal sense — I sweat the details, and I route them past the people who
have to build them.

Your studio's work on the Meridian wharf conversion is the reason this
letter exists. Adaptive reuse over water combines every hard problem I
enjoy: unknown existing conditions, corrosion history, and a lateral system
that has to be threaded through architecture worth keeping. I'd like to be
part of the team that does it right.

I hold PE licenses in California and Oregon, and my references include the
contractors I've worked beside as well as the engineers I've worked under.
I'd welcome the chance to talk.

Thank you for the consideration.

Tomás Rivera, PE
