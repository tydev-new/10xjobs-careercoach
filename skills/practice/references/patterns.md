# Practice — the craft

Hints for running the drill loop and the sequences in `../SKILL.md`
faster; the scoring standard is `eval.md`, the file shapes `schema.md`.
Read the section for the mode you are in.

## Three-tier target model (what questions come from)

1. **Fundamentals** — no role data needed: ladder stages 1–4, retrieval drill. Available to anyone, anytime.
2. **Composite target** — the *waiting mode* (pipeline exists, nothing scheduled): synthesize `composite-target.md` from the top jd-analyses — frequency-weighted competencies (evaluate's batch "market sweet spot" is exactly this) + the union of predicted-question themes + **the likely interview formats** across those roles (system design · live demo · presentation/panel · case study · coding screen — from each decode's format signals and the role archetype), naming any format two-plus targets imply that has no `courses/<slug>.md` as a **course gap**. Stamp the file with the synthesis date + source roles.
   **Staleness check — at every drill start** (the coach's Monday mirror runs the same check): if any of the composite's own triggers fired since its stamp (new strong-fit decode · a prep brief landed · verdicts/stages changed), say which fired and **propose** regeneration. Regenerate only on the candidate's confirmation; **"keep the old one" is a kept answer** — note the declined refresh on the stamp line, drill on against it labeling rounds "vs the <date> composite," and re-propose only when a NEW trigger fires, never the same one twice. **Label every composite drill as synthetic** ("your market's composite, not Company X") — answers must not overfit to a JD nobody posted.
3. **Interview-specific** — a prep brief exists: drill its predicted questions, format, and top concerns. Always re-sharpen here before a real interview.

Question tailoring at every tier: known weak dimensions, storybank gaps (gap-response by story strength: 2 → Adjacent Bridge; 1 → Reframe/Growth; none → Hypothetical with Self-Awareness), round formats. Opener / "tell me about yourself" drills target `pitch.md`'s TMAY (60–90s) variant when present — drill the actual pitch, not a generic intro; the delivery signal flows back to positioning via the debrief.

## The gated ladder (exact gates)

| Stage | Drill | Gate to advance | Prereq |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ladder (same story at 30s/60s/90s/3min) | Structure ≥3 on 3 consecutive rounds | — |
| 2 | Pushback | Credibility ≥3 under pressure | 1 |
| 3 | Pivot (question ≠ prep) | Relevance ≥3 when redirected | 2 |
| 4 | Gap ("I don't have an example") | Credibility ≥3 with honest gap handling | 2 |
| 5 | Role (specialist scrutiny) | Substance ≥3 under scrutiny | 1–3 |
| 6 | Panel (multiple personas) | All dimensions ≥3 | 1–4 |
| 7 | Stress (maximum pressure) | All dimensions ≥3 | 1–5 |
| 8 (opt) | Technical communication | Structure+Substance ≥3 | 1–3 |

Standalone: **retrieval** (10 questions, 10s each, story ID + opening line; needs 8+ **confirmed** stories — a `draft [source: …]` is a document's claim the candidate has never answered on, so it does not count toward the bar. Drills may still target one on purpose: **answering on a draft IS its confirmation** — update its Status in `storybank.md` same turn. The storybank skill owns the full protocol). A request above their stage is the loop's rule 2. **Revisit queue**: resurface a struggled drill after 2–3 sessions to test durability.

## Round protocol — the drill round, getting there

Warmup round first, explicitly unscored → state each round's objective → answer → your score first (the loop's rule 1) → collect self-assessment with a self-score → strengths-first feedback from YOUR read; if reads differ, say so explicitly — never quietly converge → record the delta → after 3+ rounds, cite the candidate's own peak ("your round-2 answer hit a 4 — make that your floor") → ONE specific change for the next round. **Interviewer's Read** per round: 1–2 highest-teaching-value moments, ≥1 positive, quoting the candidate's actual words, making the scorecard make sense ("30 seconds in I still didn't know where this was going — that's what a 2 on Structure feels like").

## Training plans (for candidates who won't self-direct)

Diagnose ONE primary weakness profile from evidence (never vibes): thin_material (storybank <60% at 4+, **counting confirmed stories only** — a strength rating on a draft rates the document, not the candidate; report drafts separately → build/improve one story per rep) · delivery (content ≥4, structure low → 60-second cuts, answer-first) · calibration (self-rating gap ≥2 → predict-before-score every rep) · positioning (fine answers, weak differentiation → pitch reps vs target framing) · avoidance (reps skipped → 5-minute reps, streak framing, never shame). Rep contract: one rep, 10–15 min, ONE thing, one fix. Timeline: count back from the interview date (final week = daily retrieval); no date → 3×/week — "daily is earned by a deadline." Two skips → serve the 5-minute version matter-of-factly ("streaks restart, the plan doesn't"). Weekly checkpoint re-tilts from evidence.

## Role-specialist drills (weekly, not just pre-interview; candidate describes real work, coach challenges by lens)

**PM Six-Lens Stress Test** (Engineering, Design, Data, Business, Competitor, Skeptic — progressively harder; scores tension-acknowledgment / evidence / uncertainty-admission) · **Engineer Technical Depth** (Architecture, Tradeoffs, Debugging, Collaboration, Edge Cases) · **Designer Critique & Rationale** · **Data Scientist Methodology Rigor** · **UX Researcher Evidence & Influence** · **Ops Systems Thinking** · **Marketing Strategy & Attribution**. Leadership candidates: run the role lens set of the teams they'd lead, at judgment altitude. Difficulty scales: rounds 1–2 open, 3–4 add constraints, 5+ hardest lenses + compound challenges. Push on "we" ("what was YOUR contribution?"); probe the negative when only positives are offered.

## Technical-communication drills (communication, not domain knowledge — learn's courses cover domain canon)

1–2 per session, not all four. **Clarification-Seeking** first (the most common failure — jumping in unscoped): deliberately ambiguous prompt, NO context, wait silently; both failure extremes count (paralysis AND over-clarifying as stalling); challenge: "I can't tell you that — what would you assume?" · **Thinking Out Loud**: stated assumptions, structure-before-detail, narrated reasoning, unprompted tradeoffs; "2 minutes in and I still don't know your recommendation." · **Mode-Switching**: behavioral↔technical pivots mid-answer, some unsignposted. · **Tradeoff Articulation**: no-right-answer decisions; challenges: stakeholder conflict, hindsight ("a year later the debt causes incidents"), "what would flip your answer?", "convince me of the opposite."

## Stress drill (gate: stages 1–5 passed)

Design around KNOWN weaknesses, not random pressure. Six layers, apply 3–4 simultaneously: time compression (60s for a 3-min answer) · hostile interviewer · curveball sequencing (domain jumps, no transitions) · information denial ("I can't tell you — use your judgment," repeatedly) · previous-answer callback ("earlier you said X, now Y — which is it?") · silence after answers (5+ seconds, then "is that your final answer?"). Frame: "designed to break your composure — don't be perfect, recover." 4–5 questions, NO inter-question debrief, 30 seconds of decompression, then **debrief the recovery, not the content**: where composure held/broke, recovery speed, verbose-vs-clipped under stress, did Structure collapse first.

## Panel archetypes (deploy 2–3, switch naturally, engineer conflicts)

Friendly Ally (rigor without pressure; danger = getting casual) · Skeptic ("how do you know?") · Silent Observer (composure without validation; danger = filling silence) · Off-Script Wanderer (depth beyond prepared stories) · Time-Pressured Exec ("30-second version") · Culture Guardian (values probing; danger = performative answers).

## Mocks — getting there

**Setup**: format (behavioral screen / deep behavioral / panel / bar raiser / system design-case / mixed) + company/role; difficulty calibrated to ladder stage (no max-intensity mock for a stage-2 candidate); tone calibrated to company (big tech = structured, metrics-heavy; startup = conversational, scrappiness; consulting = case precision).

**Execution**: one question at a time, NO feedback between; one curveball; banked real questions (`question-bank.md`) matching the company or format go in FIRST — nothing simulates an interviewer better than what one actually asked; then the prep brief's PREDICTED questions are the pool (a mock is the brief's rehearsal, not a fresh derivation — vary the wording so the candidate practices the competency, not a script), MIXED with the generic staples a real round always carries: open with tell-me-about-yourself (target: pitch.md's 60-90s variant) and include 1-2 high-signal generics from `../../prep/references/patterns.md § High-signal question patterns` — real interviews are never all role-specific; ≥1 question at a known story gap and ≥1 at the top-ranked concern (from the prep brief). **Adapt like a real interviewer** — pursue strong threads, redirect from weak ones, chase contradictions; track pursued-vs-abandoned threads as signal-reading data. Track story diversity (<80% unique = flag), energy trajectory, answer lengths. System-design/case mocks: state the boundary ("communication process, not technical correctness"), observe the clarification phase without prompting, probe tradeoffs and "constraint changed — now what?". Mixed-format: deliberate mid-question mode switch; score integration ("one candidate or two?").

**Pre-debrief self-assessment** (before ANY feedback): overall hire-signal self-read, best/worst answer, what they'd change. **One redo** of the weakest answer, scored independently, before/after shown.

**Debrief**: overall Hire Signal (Strong Hire / Hire / Mixed / No Hire) → arc analysis → per-question scorecard (5 dims + strongest moment + missed opportunity) → holistic patterns (crutch phrases, avoided topics, hesitation triggers) → signal-reading notes → **Interviewer's Inner Monologue** (quote their actual words, show real-time reactions, mark where the impression pivoted, in the company's calibrated voice) → top 3 changes. If the candidate picked their strongest format: name the avoidance — growth is in the uncomfortable mock.

## The debrief — getting there (capture-focused, not scoring-focused)

1. **Emotional check first** — "How are you feeling about it? One word." Don't skip. Triage: feels good → proceed; feels terrible → capture only, defer analysis, offer the day-of toolkit, human-support aside; **uncertain → "actually the most valuable state"** — capture and let the data speak.
2. Rapid question capture ("don't worry about exact wording"; prompt with format cues) — **every question lands in `question-bank.md` same-day** (`schema.md`); approximate wording is fine and marked so. (Vague stays vague — the debrief's rule 2.)
3. Per-question self-read: strong / okay / rough — don't score yet; the read lands in that question's `question-bank.md` row (`read` column).
4. **Signal reading** (directional, never verdicts): extended follow-ups = interest or depth-check, positive either way (HIGH) · "let me push back on that" = testing conviction, often positive (HIGH) · interviewer selling the role = strong buy signal (HIGH) · quick move-ons = satisfied OR didn't land — read the energy (MED) · short closed follow-ups = assessment likely formed (MED) · "that's interesting" + a real follow-up = engagement (HIGH) · clock-checking = schedule pressure, not boredom — repeated late in the slot = you're running long (MED) · "we'll be in touch" = nothing (LOW).
5. Surprise capture — unexpected questions/format ("often the most informative for coaching").
6. **Round-intel capture** — three routes, same turn: **(a)** comp discussed? a number STATED? → `negotiation/<company>.md` (a stated number must stay consistent across rounds; prep's day-of sheet reads it) · **(b)** any fact this round contradicted or confirmed (comp band, team size, stack, reporting line, location policy) → correct or resolve it at its SOURCE file (`company/<slug>.md`, `jd-analysis/`), dated — re-prep regenerates from those files, so an uncorrected fact re-enters the next brief · **(c)** anything about how THEY ran the round that worries the candidate about working there → dated culture note in `company/<slug>.md`; a pattern across 2+ rounds is the coach's to surface · **(d)** "anything you got pushed on and couldn't defend?" — a conceded claim is struck NOW: a ⚠ never line in `base-resume.md § Claim rules` (direction rule — profile owns the file), the struck form in double quotes + what to say instead; every drafting checker already enforces that list, so the concession becomes a gate the same day.
7. **Story usage log** → update `storybank.md` Use Counts + Last Used + performance notes (prep's freshness checks read these). A NEW story the candidate told in the interview is captured now, in storybank's record format (`../../storybank/references/schema.md`), status `confirmed` — they just told it live; real interviews are a story source in their own right — capture it now.
8. Candidate's own tactical notes for next round.
9. **How did the TMAY land?** → feeds the positioning skill's pitch iteration.
10. Recruiter comments, however informal.
11. Similarity check vs. `question-bank.md` — only surface useful matches.
12. Transcript available? → run Analysis (below); the felt-vs-actual gap is prime coaching data. No transcript → lighter directional read, flagged: "I wouldn't hang precise scores on this."

The four writes that close a debrief are the rule in `../SKILL.md § The debrief`; the capture file, bank rows, round record, and use counts are shaped in `schema.md`.

## Feedback intake (inbound information between workflows — capture, not analysis)

- **Recruiter/interviewer feedback**: record near-verbatim ("paraphrasing loses signal"); map to dimensions *lightly* (some feedback is external factor, not coaching gap). **"External feedback is higher-signal than internal scoring"** — a contradiction with coach assessment is a drift signal; 2+ on the same dimension → flag for the next progress review's calibration check.
- **Outcome report** (advanced/rejected/offer/withdrawn): log it; "advanced" → ask "do you know what's next — format, timeline, interviewer?" and land the answer in the round record (continuation prep reads it); at 3+ real outcomes, practice-vs-reality correlation becomes possible (profile's progress mode). Rejection at full directness: lead with extraction, not comfort — "Rejection is data. This data says [specific insight]." At gentler settings: empathy first, extraction second.
- **Coaching correction** (candidate disputes a score): three honest outcomes — warranted (adjust), calibration gap (hold the line with evidence, log the data point), ambiguous (name both reads). "No defensiveness, no rubber-stamping."
- **Late memories** → route to the right file (question, story, signal, culture note).
- **Meta-feedback on the coaching** → adjust directness/strategy; note it.

## Transcript analysis — getting there (real recordings — Otter/Grain/Zoom VTT)

Score each answer on the 5 dimensions (`eval.md`, at the candidate's band), format-aware. **Comp-call detector**: 3+ salary markers → this was a negotiation, not an interview — "success is the package outcome, not answer quality" — capture what was said and DON'T score it as an interview (comp coaching is not in this system: say so plainly rather than improvising a number). Then: self-assessment-vs-scores calibration; **triage priority stack** — Relevance > Substance > Structure > Credibility > Differentiation (can't fix downstream before upstream); "all 3s = a ceiling problem → differentiation work"; proactive rewrite of the weakest answer; Interviewer's-Inner-Monologue replay on pivotal moments; new questions → `question-bank.md`, same capture rules as debrief step 2.

## Proposing a new pattern

When a drill variant, a lens, or a debrief prompt proves itself across
two candidates' sessions, write it as a dated line at the end of the
session file it came from — name the two sessions. A pattern is never self-adopted; a human promotes it into this file.
