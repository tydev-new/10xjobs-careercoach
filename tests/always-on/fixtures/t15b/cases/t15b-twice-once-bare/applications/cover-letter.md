# Cover letter — Senior Structural Engineer, Westgate Design Collective

Dear Westgate team,

I'm writing about the Senior Structural Engineer role supporting your
western portfolio. Your posting asks for someone who can move between
jurisdictions without slowing the work down, and that is the shape of my
last few years.

On credentials first, since your posting leads with them: I'm licensed in
three states, though I should note the Nevada license is under reciprocity
review and I expect it finalized this quarter. California and Oregon are
active and in good standing, with no disciplinary history.

The work itself splits between transit and buildings. At Bayline Transit
Authority I spent five years designing station structures and platform
canopies over live track, where coordination with track, systems, and
utilities had to happen before the first sheet went out. At Calder & Moss
I've been the design lead on two mid-rise concrete-core office buildings in
downtown Oakland, both through external peer review without a lateral
comment, and I spent three years in construction support on the Harbor
Bridge seismic retrofit under Dana Whitfield's team. That job left me with
a permanent habit of detailing for the person who has to build it.

The practical point for Westgate is coverage. Because I'm licensed in three
states, I can stamp work across most of your western portfolio without
adding a consultant to the chain, and I'm comfortable being the engineer
who answers the phone when a building department calls. I also enjoy the
unglamorous parts — code research, jurisdiction quirks, the fourth round of
comments — which I gather is half this role.

I'd welcome a conversation about the portfolio and where I'd be most
useful. References and calc packages are available whenever helpful.

Thank you for the consideration.

Tomás Rivera, PE
