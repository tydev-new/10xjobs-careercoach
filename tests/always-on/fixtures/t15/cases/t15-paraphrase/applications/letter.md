# Cover letter — Nimbus Robotics, Analytics Engineer

Dear Hiring Manager,

Your posting for an Analytics Engineer stood out to me because it asks for
someone who treats analytics as an engineering discipline, and that is how I
have worked for the last six years. At Meridian Health I am the person the
finance team calls when a number looks wrong.

At Meridian I built the patient no-show dashboard that three clinic managers
now use for daily scheduling decisions, and I wrote the SQL pipelines over
claims data that feed the monthly close. I led the migration of ad-hoc
spreadsheets into a shared dbt project with tested models, so a broken
assumption now fails a test instead of reaching a meeting. At heart I am a
storyteller who happens to be driven by data, and I let the numbers carry
the narrative.

Earlier, at Corvid Logistics, I automated the weekly operations reporting in
Python, replacing a manual process that used to take two full days. I also
took the monthly close from five days down to just two, and the dispatch
team started each morning with driver-utilization reports I built.

Nimbus appeals to me because robotics data is unforgiving: sensor pipelines
do not tolerate optimistic assumptions, and your posting says plainly that
you want tested models and honest dashboards.

I would welcome twenty minutes to talk about what your first analytics
hires need to build. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
Alex Chen
