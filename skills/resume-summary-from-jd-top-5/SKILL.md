# Self-contained resume summary from JD evidence

## Goal
Make the candidate's strongest case for this role while remaining understandable to a reader who has not seen the job description. The JD selects evidence; it is not the outline the reader must reconstruct.

## Process
1. Read the captured JD, candidate profile, approved claims and current resume. Privately map the top 3-5 responsibilities or requirements to the strongest approved evidence. Keep this mapping in preparation/review notes, not as public summary labels. Flag unsupported must-haves honestly.
2. Write a short, self-contained introduction using distinct full-sentence bullets (usually 3-4, not a fixed count). Begin with what the candidate personally builds or owns, and explain an unfamiliar product and its purpose before relying on its name. Establish employer/role context where it helps.
3. Select supporting points that explain personal ownership, mechanisms and outcomes. Use the strongest relevant approved numbers, without cramming every metric or technology into the first bullet. One primary idea per bullet; combine related details rather than repeat tools, routing or outcomes.
4. Explain changes of context. When moving between employers, product domains or kinds of systems, state what the work actually was and why the evidence matters. Do not imply earlier infrastructure work was AI or security work. Do not add defensive disclaimers such as 'not LLM or security' to the resume; describe the real work positively and precisely.
5. Keep jargon only when it adds useful specificity and provide enough context for it. Do not require short lead labels or force one public bullet per JD ask. No generic strength catalog, keyword dump, unexplained product names, search-note voice, JD paraphrases as standalone labels, or duplicated Core Strengths block.
6. Optional location/work-authorization line belongs separately at the end if useful; it does not count as evidence of fit.
7. Review in two passes: first read the summary WITHOUT the JD and check whether a reader can explain what the candidate builds, what the product does, what they owned and what improved; then compare with the private requirement-to-proof map to ensure all strongest reasons for this role remain high and plain. Fix lost coverage by improving the story or placing proof in experience, not by blindly adding a label or fifth bullet.
8. Run current mechanical precheck and the independent combined review specified by the application workflow. This skill does not replace claim verification, best_candidate analysis, rejection-reason handling, review freshness or approval requirements.

## Boundaries
Use only approved facts, titles, technologies and metrics. Distinguish adjacent experience from direct experience. No new claims or base-resume changes without approval. A dry run remains a preview until the user authorizes applying it. Do not submit an application as part of summary writing.
