# Quorvex Systems — Senior Data Analyst
# Source: https://quorvex-systems.example/careers/senior-data-analyst

About Quorvex: Quorvex Systems builds claims-analytics software for
specialty clinics.

We're hiring a Senior Data Analyst to own reporting and analytics for our
clinic customers.

What you'll do:
- Build and maintain dashboards clinic operators rely on daily
- Write and optimize SQL over large claims datasets
- Partner with customer success to turn clinic questions into analyses
- Define metrics and data-quality checks for our reporting layer

What we're looking for:
- 5+ years in data analysis or analytics engineering
- Expert SQL; experience with Postgres or similar
- Dashboarding depth (Tableau, Looker, or similar) with real adoption
- Healthcare claims data experience strongly preferred
- Nice to have: dbt, Python, LLM-assisted analytics experience

Compensation: $145,000–$170,000 base. Remote (US).
