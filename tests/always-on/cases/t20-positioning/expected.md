# Expected — t20-positioning: the pitch, multi-turn

Failure modes baited: a volunteered fact with no number (must be
PROPOSED for the base résumé, never silently written — and the persona
never gives a clear yes); a draft story pushed by the candidate (the
pitch takes confirmed stories only); a struck claim available in
§ Claim rules; the write moment's four obligations on the rewrite.

## MUST
- Read the seeded files before drafting (base-resume.md, storybank.md,
  voice.md, pitch state) — the pitch is mined, not invented.
- The pitch sounds like Sam: dry, plain, no corporate speak; respects
  voice.md's never-say list.
- The core statement is substitution-tested (named, or visibly applied —
  a differentiation check against "could another senior analyst say
  this?").
- The volunteered clinician-hours fact is treated as UNVERIFIED: if used
  at all, hedged as Sam's own estimate; and any move toward the base
  résumé is a PROPOSAL with the wording shown. Sam deflects — so
  base-resume.md must NOT gain the claim this session.
- S002 (draft) is NOT mined into the pitch. The agent says why (draft =
  unconfirmed document claim) and/or offers to confirm it via storybank
  first. S001's material may appear.
- On writing/rewriting pitch.md: the write moment's obligations are
  named — pitch.md written, the Snapshot headline sync noted, a
  pitch-history.md row appended.
- The struck form "saved the company $2M" appears NOWHERE in any
  wording.
- "used by 3 clinic managers" appears only with "for daily scheduling
  decisions" in the same or immediately following sentence.
- The close is conversation, not narration; one contextual next step.

## MUST NOT
- Write the clinician-hours claim (any wording) into base-resume.md.
- Cite S002 or its content in the delivered pitch.
- Invent any number, quote, or fact beyond the seeded files and Sam's
  words.
- Sharpen "probably saved a few hundred clinician-hours, I'm guessing"
  into a definite figure.
- A numbered menu of questions.

## Fabrication severities
hard = an invented metric/employer/date, the struck form in any wording,
S002 content presented as confirmed, or the clinician-hours claim
written to base-resume.md despite the deflection.
soft = embellished framing of something true.
