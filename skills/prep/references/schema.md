# Prep — the file shape

One file per role, `prep/<company_key>-<title_key>.md` — the key is
the job's from `jobs.md`, deterministic. ONE canonical brief,
overwritten on re-prep, no versioned copies: questions, concerns, and
the day-of sheet are computed once and reused from here (a day-of
session pulls the brief rather than regenerating — regeneration risks
contradictory advice). Read by practice (mocks and drills reuse the
format and the mapping), by outreach (thank-you context), and by the
coach's inbox sweep (the header line is where a captured interview date
lands). No script parses it; the section names below are the ones the
written briefs already share.

## `prep/<company_key>-<title_key>.md`

- **Header line** — `<Company> — <Role> · round: <type> · date:
  <date/TBD>`. Kept current on re-prep.
- `## Interview format` — incl. platform (video / phone / onsite;
  unstated → "confirm before the call") and any discovered format
  details, saved so mocks and drills don't re-ask.
- `## Company culture read` — sourced, with "what I don't know".
- `## Interviewer intelligence` — if any; each insight says profile
  says vs inferred.
- `## What they optimize for` — top 3.
- `## Your best positioning` — one line + proof + the earned secret to
  deploy.
- `## Fit + likely concerns & counters` — 3, each with evidence, ranked
  Dealbreaker / Significant / Minor.
- `## Storybank health` — count, strength distribution, drafts reported
  separately, overuse, freshness.
- `## Predicted questions` — 7–10, **each labeled with its tier**:
  `Asked — <company>, R<N>, <date>` (banked; `rough` marked) · a cite
  (researched — workspace file or a source fetched this session) ·
  `[inferred]` (generated). The searches that ran are stated, so "none
  found" is verifiable.
- `## Story mapping` — `question | primary story + fit | backup + fit |
  notes`, fit one of Strong Fit / Workable / Stretch / Gap; a `draft`
  story is labeled `draft` in the table; then portfolio health (unique
  stories used, conflicts resolved, strength/freshness/overuse
  warnings) and gaps with their named pattern.
- `## Questions to ask` — 5, each with its purpose, round, likely
  reversal, and the prepared response.
- `## Questions to avoid this round` — 1–2, specific to this candidate.
- `## Confidence` — plus the unknowns reducing it.
- `## Day-of cheat sheet` — closes the file: the 60-second reel, the
  pre-call 3×3 (top 3 concerns + counters, top 3 questions, THE one
  question for this interviewer), the focus cue, the warm-up — and,
  when `negotiation/<company>.md` records a stated number, "already
  stated $X to <who> on <date>: stay consistent."

## What prep reads and never writes

`jd-analysis/` and `company/` (evaluate's; reuse, never re-derive) ·
`storybank.md` + `stories/` · `contacts/<company>.md` (outreach's dated
hooks — reuse the fresh ones) · `knowledge.md` (learn's map) ·
`question-bank.md` and `practice-log.md` (practice's — the bank is the sourcing ladder's top tier) · `negotiation/<company>.md` · the cover letter for this job (the case already argued) · `base-resume.md § Claim rules` (a struck claim never reaches the brief).
