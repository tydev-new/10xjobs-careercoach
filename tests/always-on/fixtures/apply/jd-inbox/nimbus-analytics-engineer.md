# Nimbus Robotics — Analytics Engineer (raw JD, saved 2026-08-15)

Source: https://jobs.nimbusrobotics.example/analytics-engineer (fictional fixture)

## About the role

Nimbus Robotics builds warehouse automation. Our fleet emits millions of telemetry events a day, and the company runs on the numbers our data team publishes. You will own the analytics layer: dbt models, pipeline reliability, and the daily metrics our ops and finance teams make decisions with.

## What you'll do

- Own our dbt project: model design, tests, and code review for the analytics layer.
- Build and operate reliable pipelines — you'll join the data on-call rotation and run data-quality incident response.
- Partner with warehouse ops to turn their morning questions into metrics they trust.
- Set the planning cadence for the analytics backlog with the data lead.

## Who you are

- 5+ years in analytics or data engineering.
- Expert SQL; dbt in production; solid Python.
- You have operated pipelines, not just built them — on-call, incidents, postmortems.
- You bring data and hold the line when a stakeholder wants a prettier number.
- Bonus: healthcare or logistics domain; LLM tooling experience.

## Logistics

Hybrid, Oakland HQ, 3 days a week in office. Salary $150-185k.
