# The No-Show Dashboard That Won Over the Clinics

## The Short Version

When I joined Meridian Health, clinic managers were flying blind on patient
no-shows. I built a Tableau dashboard over our Postgres claims data that
surfaced no-show patterns by clinic, day, and provider. It was adopted by
all 12 clinic managers across the network and became part of their daily
standup routine. The rollout taught me that adoption comes from sitting
with the users, not from better charts.

## Earned Secret

Dashboards win adoption in the user's meeting, not in the BI tool —
I redesigned it twice based on watching managers try to use it live.
