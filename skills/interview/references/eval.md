# Interview — how to judge the work, and the scoring standard

`SKILL.md` names the destination; this file defines the rubrics and verification checks for both role prep briefs and live interview drilling.

## Who checks what

- **Structure and manifest → `../../profile/scripts/check_files.py`**: checks `prep/`, `practice/`, `practice-log.md`, `question-bank.md`, `composite-target.md`, `negotiation/`.
- **Language against written rules → no checker-subagent**: scores, briefs, and feedback are the coach's voice.
- **Semantic quality → verified during turn**: sourced claims, question tiers, story fit accuracy, independent scoring.

## Prep brief evaluation

- **Header and section integrity:** Every section present, current metadata line, day-of cheat sheet closing the brief.
- **Question tiering:** Banked questions outrank generated; researched carry inline citations; inferred marked `[inferred]`.
- **Sourced intelligence:** Company and interviewer insights tiered (verified / general knowledge / unknown). No invented panel or process facts.
- **Story mapping:** Confirmed stories only (`Strong Fit`, `Workable`, `Stretch`, `Gap`). Gaps use named handling patterns.
- **Reverse questions:** 5 questions with purpose, round, and reversal triggers.

## Live drill & mock scoring rubric

### The 5 dimensions (anchors at 1 / 3 / 5)

- **Substance:** 1 generic platitudes · 3 specific claim, unquantified · 5 quantified + alternatives weighed + rationale + impact.
- **Structure:** 1 stream of consciousness · 3 mechanical STAR · 5 crisp setup $\rightarrow$ conflict $\rightarrow$ resolution $\rightarrow$ impact.
- **Relevance:** 1 fails to answer question · 3 right story with noise · 5 direct, indispensable framing.
- **Credibility:** 1 ungrounded/exaggerated claims · 3 specific details without metrics · 5 numbers + artifacts + constraints + third-party validation.
- **Differentiation:** 1 generic response · 3 real details without insight · 5 unmistakable candidate thesis + earned secrets.

### Seniority band calibration

- **Early career (0–3 yrs):** Specific examples + metrics; learning velocity and self-awareness.
- **Mid-level (3–7 yrs):** Independent ownership, technical depth, cross-functional tradeoffs.
- **Senior / Lead (7–12 yrs):** Architecture, ambiguous problem scoping, team-level leverage, failure post-mortems.
- **Staff / Principal / Executive (12+ yrs):** Strategic alignment, organizational transformation, earned secrets, multi-quarter vision.

### Root-cause taxonomy

1. **Retrieval failure:** Wrong story picked.
2. **Structure collapse:** Rambling or missing resolution.
3. **Band misalignment:** Acting at the wrong seniority altitude.
4. **Vulnerability deficit:** Sanitizing challenges or avoiding accountability.
5. **Differentiation void:** Giving the standard textbook answer.
