# Job-search storybank

Workspace default: `<search-workspace>/`.

## Goal
Stories they own, for pitches, bullets, and interviews. Not a filled-in form.

## What you own
Follow `operating-rules.md` when present. You choose the words. A story is a hard moment, what they did, one real number, and a lesson they would still argue for. A big change is 60 to 90 seconds, with detail only if they ask. This skill owns the coverage map, `stories/`, `storybank.md`, confirmed only when they told it, the improve loop, and retrieval drills. STAR (situation, task, action, result) is optional. File every strong chat draft in `stories/`. Do not leave the only copy in the chat.

## Coverage map
Leadership and group size, conflict, a real failure, a delivery or numbers turnaround, customer or enterprise work, influence across teams, technical depth, and feedback given or received.

## Capture
1. Pick the biggest gap.
2. Ask one question at a time: the hard moment, what they did, one number, the lesson.
3. Write `stories/S###-<slug>.md` and index it in `storybank.md`. Status is `confirmed` only if they told it.
4. Propose a new resume fact. Do not write it into the base resume or claims yourself.

### Level
Engineering manager: one team. Director: how several teams work. Vice president: the portfolio, and whether people trust the plan. Raise the level only with real facts.

## Improve
Score 1 to 5: hard moment, ownership, a defensible number, a sticky lesson, and level. At 3 or below, try at most 2 or 3 rounds. Two rounds with no movement is a decision.

## STAR
Use STAR only if they or the interviewer ask. Plain speech first.

## Retrieval drill
With 6 or more confirmed stories, ask which story fits a question. Log the rounds in `practice-log.md`.
