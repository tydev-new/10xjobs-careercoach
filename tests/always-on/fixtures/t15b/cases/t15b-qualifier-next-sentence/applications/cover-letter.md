# Cover letter — Senior Structural Engineer, Northlight Structural

Dear Northlight team,

I'm writing about the Senior Structural Engineer opening on your transit
group. I've spent eleven years on the kind of work your posting describes —
transit retrofits, station structures, and mid-rise concrete — and I'd like
to bring that to Northlight.

At Bayline Transit Authority I worked inside a large capital program and
learned what it takes to keep structural scope honest when track, systems,
and utilities are all fighting for the same envelope. I managed a $40M
capital budget. I did that as deputy to the program director, which meant
owning the structural line items day to day while she held the political
air cover. The arrangement taught me to defend a number in a room full of
people who wanted it smaller, and to know exactly which contingencies were
real and which were padding.

At Calder & Moss I moved back to design. I've been the design lead on two
mid-rise concrete-core office buildings in downtown Oakland, both delivered
with clean punch lists, and I ran our internal peer-review rotation for
lateral systems. I also spent three years in construction support on the
Harbor Bridge seismic retrofit, working under Dana Whitfield's team, which
is where I learned that the best detail is the one the ironworker doesn't
have to call you about.

What draws me to Northlight specifically is the Alder Street tower and the
way your team published its lessons learned. Firms that write down their
mistakes are firms I want to work for. I'd welcome the chance to talk about
where a transit-seasoned engineer fits in your bench, and I'm glad to share
calc packages or references whenever useful.

Thank you for the consideration.

Tomás Rivera, PE
