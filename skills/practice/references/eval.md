# Practice — how to judge the work, and the scoring standard

`SKILL.md` names the destination; this file is how to tell it is
reached, who checks, and the rubric every scored round uses.

## Who checks what

- **Structure and counts → `../../profile/scripts/check_files.py`**:
  the manifest (`practice-log.md`, `question-bank.md`,
  `composite-target.md`, `practice/`). Practice has no script of its
  own.
- **Language against written rules → no checker-subagent.** Scores and
  feedback are the coach's voice; the one candidate-voiced write —
  a struck form into `§ Claim rules` — is a ruling, checked by every
  drafting checker downstream.
- **Everything semantic → you, at the moment**: the score at the band,
  the root cause, whether a recall is vague or crisp.

## The destination, judged

- **Every scored round was scored independently BEFORE the candidate said anything**, the band stated, the self-assessment delta logged — never smoothed. An over-rater has blind spots; an under-rater pays a self-doubt tax; a near-zero delta across many sessions means the coach may be anchoring — score the next one blind first.
- **Every scored session left two writes**: the session file and the
  Scoreboard row on top, linking it.
- **A real interview left four** (t12-debrief-bank measures this —
  zero writes was the failure): the capture file, the Scoreboard round
  record, every question in `question-bank.md`, the story use counts.
- **Vague stayed vague** in the bank, and **no numeric score was hung
  on recall** — without a transcript the read is directional, flagged.
- **Gates were gates**: a jump was named with its risk, then respected;
  warmups were never scored; composite drills were labeled synthetic
  every time.
- **Real-interview data outranked practice scores**; a contradiction
  was logged as a drift signal.

## The five dimensions (anchors at 1 / 3 / 5)

- **Substance**: 1 generic platitude ("I'm a great collaborator") · 3
  specific claim, unquantified · 5 quantified + alternatives weighed +
  decision rationale + outcome.
- **Structure**: 1 stream of consciousness · 3 clear structure,
  mechanical transitions (good-but-choppy STAR) · 5 crisp
  setup→conflict→resolution→impact — "every sentence advances the
  story."
- **Relevance**: 1 doesn't address the question asked · 3 right story,
  ~40% background noise · 5 "could not remove a single sentence without
  losing value."
- **Credibility**: 1 unsupported/exaggerated ("single-handedly
  transformed the culture") · 3 specific details, no numbers/outcomes ·
  5 numbers + artifacts + third-party validation + realistic
  constraints.
- **Differentiation**: 1 any prepared candidate could say it · 3 real
  details, no earned insight · 5 "unmistakably this candidate" — earned
  secrets + a defensible stance. Watch: "the biggest enemy of
  differentiation is AI-polished prep."

## Seniority calibration — state the band with every score

- **Early career (0–3 yrs)**: a "4 on Substance" = specific examples
  with at least one metric; differentiation from learning velocity and
  curiosity; look for self-awareness about what they don't yet know.
- **Mid-career (4–8)**: "4 on Substance" = quantified impact with
  alternatives considered; differentiation needs genuine earned secrets
  from hands-on work; ownership of outcomes, not tasks.
- **Senior/Lead (8–15)**: "4 on Substance" = systems-level thinking —
  second-order effects, organizational impact; differentiation =
  insights that reshape how the interviewer thinks; judgment across
  ambiguous tradeoffs.
- **Executive (15+)**: "4 on Substance" = business-level impact with P&L
  awareness; differentiation = a coherent leadership philosophy backed
  by pattern recognition across contexts; builds through others.

## Aggregate, trends, and the ladder's gates

Interview-level: **Strong Hire** (multiple 4–5s, no major gaps, unique
value) / **Hire** (mostly 3–4s, coachable) / **Mixed** / **No Hire**.
Trend thresholds per dimension: improving +0.5 from baseline · stagnant
±0.3 · declining −0.5. **Stagnant after 3+ sessions signals the need
to change approach, not just practice more.** The ladder's gates
(`patterns.md § The gated ladder`) are the per-stage standard: a stage
is cleared when its named dimension holds ≥3 on the stated number of
rounds.

## Root-cause taxonomy — diagnose the pattern, prescribe ONE intervention

1. Can't identify the question's core → question-decoding drills
   (restate what's really asked)
2. Reflexive "we" framing → I/we audit (name the specific individual
   actions)
3. Conflict avoidance → tension-mining (make the hardest moment the
   centerpiece)
4. Status anxiety / over-claiming → constraint practice (add limits,
   timelines, tradeoffs). Fabrication is "a red flag, not a coaching
   gap."
5. Narrative hoarding → constraint ladder (30/60/90-second versions)
6. Fear of being wrong → spiky-POV practice (take and defend a stance)
7. Performance anxiety → psychological readiness (warmups,
   mid-interview recovery)
8. Cultural communication style → adaptation coaching — "a style
   difference, not a skill deficit"
9. Linguistic formality → gentle register calibration; clarity over idiom

Same root cause across 2+ sessions (or 2+ answers in one) → escalate to
a tracked entry with one unified treatment — never parallel
per-dimension drills for one underlying cause.
