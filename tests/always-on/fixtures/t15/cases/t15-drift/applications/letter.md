# Cover letter — Nimbus Robotics, Analytics Engineer

Dear Hiring Manager,

Your posting for an Analytics Engineer stood out to me because it asks for
someone who treats analytics as an engineering discipline, and that is how I
have worked for the last six years. At Meridian Health I am the person the
finance and operations teams call when a number looks wrong, and I would like
to bring that same reliability focus to a robotics data platform.

At Meridian I built the patient no-show dashboard that three clinic managers
now use for daily scheduling decisions, and I wrote the SQL pipelines over
claims data that feed the monthly close. When I arrived, most reporting lived
in ad-hoc spreadsheets that broke quietly and often. I led the migration into
a shared dbt project with tested models, which means a broken assumption now
fails a test instead of reaching a meeting. As a data-driven storyteller, I
believe the numbers should carry the narrative on their own.

Earlier, at Corvid Logistics, I automated the weekly operations reporting in
Python, replacing a manual process that used to take two full days. I also
cut the monthly close from five days to two, and the dispatch team started
each morning with driver-utilization reports I built.

Nimbus appeals to me specifically because robotics data is unforgiving:
sensor pipelines do not tolerate optimistic assumptions, and your posting
says plainly that you want tested models and honest dashboards. That is the
kind of team I do my best work on.

I would welcome twenty minutes to talk about what your first analytics
hires need to build. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
Alex Chen
