# Prep — how to judge the work

`SKILL.md` names the destination; this file is how to tell it is
reached, and who checks.

## Who checks what

- **Structure and counts → `../../profile/scripts/check_files.py`**:
  the manifest (`prep/`) at session close. Prep has no script of its
  own; nothing in the brief is a format a parser should own.
- **Language against written rules → no checker-subagent.** The brief
  is the coach's voice to the candidate, not the candidate's voice to
  anyone; `language-check.md` has nothing to check here.
- **Everything semantic → you, at the moment**: whether a claim is
  sourced or dressed up, whether a banked question outranks a
  generated one, whether a story fits or merely matches a word.

## The destination, judged

- **The brief is on file**, every section present, the header line
  current, the day-of sheet closing it.
- **Every predicted question carries its tier** — banked (`Asked — …`)
  first and outranking everything below; researched with its cite;
  generated `[inferred]`. A banked `rough` question is the top prep
  priority with a full counter, not a listed prediction. An empty bank
  says so ("0 banked — first interview at this company"), never padded
  (t12-ladder-rank measures this).
- **Nothing was sharpened** — a vague signal ("they go deep on system
  design") is reported vague; a specific question built from it is
  tier 3 with its label, never dressed as sourced
  (t12-never-sharpen).
- **Every company and interviewer claim is sourced** — verified /
  general knowledge / unknown — and nothing external was asserted
  without a source fetched this session; no invented round counts,
  panels, or culture claims.
- **Story mapping fabricated nothing**: a gap got a named pattern, a
  draft story is labeled, a story used in a prior round at this
  company is downgraded and flagged.
- **The satellites reused the brief** — questions, concerns, the day-of
  sheet pulled from it, not regenerated.

## Fit levels — the mapping standard

**Strong Fit** — primary skill matches, strength 4+, domain aligns,
secret relevant · **Workable** — secondary-skill match, OR primary at
strength 3, OR adjacent domain; needs framing guidance (secondary
matches are Workable AT BEST) · **Stretch** — reframe-able, strength
2+, heavy bridging · **Gap** — nothing at any level → a named
gap-handling pattern. Factor priority: competency match (primary >
secondary > reframe) → story strength (a strength-5 secondary can beat
a strength-3 primary) → domain alignment → freshness → variety.

## Concern ranks

**Dealbreaker** — could single-handedly end the candidacy ·
**Significant** — will come up; needs a full counter · **Minor** —
probe-only; a one-liner suffices. Every Significant+ concern carries
three framings (direct · subtle probe · follow-up challenge) and the
hard moments in exact words.
