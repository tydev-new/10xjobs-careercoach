# Voice — Tomás Rivera

Structural engineer, plain and direct. Writes the way he stamps drawings:
nothing he can't defend under questioning. Short sentences. Concrete loads,
spans, and dollar figures only when verified.

## Never-say list

- "seismic guru" — title inflation; the projects should speak, not the label.
- "detail-oriented team player" — filler that says nothing a calc package can't.

## Tone notes

Prefers first person, active voice. No exclamation points. Humor is fine but
dry; enthusiasm shows up as specificity, not adjectives.
