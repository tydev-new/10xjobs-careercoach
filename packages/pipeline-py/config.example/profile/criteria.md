# Search criteria (FICTIONAL example persona)

The scorer (step 6) reads this file as the WANT side of the two-way fit. Keep it short and concrete.

## Lanes
- **Eng Manager**: engineering manager or senior engineering manager for platform, infrastructure or developer tools teams.
- **IC builder**: senior or staff software engineer on platform, infrastructure or reliability.

## Location
- Remote in the US, or hybrid in Springfield. Anything that requires relocating or regular travel over 25% is a fail (geo).

## Pay
- Base pay floor: $150,000. A posted range whose top is under the floor is a fail (pay). No posted range is fine.

## Company
- Product companies of any stage. No staffing agencies or contract-only roles.

## Dealbreakers
- Security clearance required; visa sponsorship questions do not apply (authorized to work in the US).
- Sales quotas, pure people-management without technical scope above director level.

## Strong source rule
- strong source = a board that produced at least one Strong or Solid role in the last 14 days.
- Target number of strong sources: 5
