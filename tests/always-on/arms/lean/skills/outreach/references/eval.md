# Outreach — how to judge the work

`SKILL.md` names the destination; this file is how to tell it is
reached, and who checks.

## Who checks what

- **Structure and counts → `scripts/check_messages.py`** — the
  mechanical floor on the drafts in a contacts file: aggregate year
  counts, arrow glyphs, draft scoping, channel length notes, the
  rubric-PINNED state (a PROPOSED rubric is a WARN — pinning is a state
  check, not a language read). `../../profile/scripts/check_files.py`
  checks the manifest at session close.
- **Language against written rules → an INDEPENDENT checker-subagent**
  on `../../profile/references/language-check.md` with the drafts,
  `voice.md`, `base-resume.md § Claim rules`, and `pitch.md`'s rubric —
  never-say, WATCH-tier struck forms, and any rewording of either
  (t15; #29).
- **Everything semantic → you, at the moment**: whether a hook is the
  recipient's own, whether the ask is small, whether it sounds like the
  candidate.

Every FAIL and fix-before-delivery flag is fixed before a draft is
shown; WARNs are defended in the reply.

## The destination, judged

- **Every lead carries its evidence chain, its confidence, and a named
  send channel** — a lead with no channel is not a lead; a title from
  an article is a hypothesis until the person's own profile or a
  first-party page confirms it. Org and team names are JD/ATS-truth,
  quoted exactly or omitted.
- **Every draft has a sourced hook or a recorded miss** — the
  recipient's own post/talk/project (identity-linked, dated), or the
  explicit line "no recent signals found — company-level hook used".
  Never person A's post in a message to person B.
- **Every draft carries its written rubric line** — a check that leaves
  no record didn't happen.
- **The ceiling was honest** (t13-ceiling): at two passes without
  clearing the bar, the rubric line says UNMET and names what didn't
  fit, and the tradeoff went to the candidate.
- **Nothing was sent**; every send the candidate made is logged, dated,
  by channel.

## The rubric — five criteria, scored on every draft

**Specificity** (references THEIR post/project/talk — not
could-be-sent-to-anyone) · **Brevity** (every sentence earns its place)
· **Ask clarity** (single, small, easy to say yes to; "give me a job" is
an automatic Weak) · **Value signal** (why should THEY care — entirely
me-centered = Weak) · **Authenticity** (the candidate's voice — when
`voice.md` exists, mirror its samples and respect its never-say list;
template/AI-sounding = rewrite).

## Channel limits — the contracts

Connection request **300 characters** · cold email **75–125 words** ·
InMail under 400 characters (subject 28–39) · thank-you note **<120
words** · post-meeting follow-up <150 words · hiring-post response
~300 characters if not connected. Every word over a limit costs
response probability; the limit is part of the standard, not a
suggestion.
