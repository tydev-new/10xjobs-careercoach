# Interview — the file shapes

What the files must hold across role prep briefs, practice logs, question banks, and session captures.

## `prep/<company_key>-<title_key>.md` — role prep brief

- **Header line** — `<Company> — <Role> · round: <type> · date: <date/TBD>`
- `## Interview format` — platform + format details
- `## Company culture read` — sourced read + unknown gaps
- `## Interviewer intelligence` — professional background intelligence
- `## What they optimize for` — top 3 signals
- `## Your best positioning` — one line + proof + earned secret
- `## Fit + likely concerns & counters` — 3 ranked concerns with counters
- `## Storybank health` — story readiness, strength distribution, gaps
- `## Predicted questions` — 7–10 questions categorized: banked, researched with cites, generated `[inferred]`
- `## Story mapping` — question to story mapping (`Strong Fit` / `Workable` / `Stretch` / `Gap`)
- `## Questions to ask` — 5 reverse questions with purpose, round, and reversal trigger
- `## Questions to avoid this round` — 1–2 role/stage specific caveats
- `## Confidence` — confidence score + caveats
- `## Day-of cheat sheet` — 60s reel, 3×3 cheat sheet, focus cue, warm-up, and stated comp numbers

## `practice-log.md` — the Scoreboard, latest row FIRST

Append-only table front-loaded at the top of the file, newest row on top:
`| Date | Mode | Target | Score (per dimension + band used) | Delta (self-assessment) | Root cause | One change | Transcript | Outcome |`

## `question-bank.md` — real questions only

`| date | company | round | question | competency | read | notes |`
Exclusively holds questions actually asked of THIS candidate in real interviews (`read` = `strong` / `okay` / `rough`).

## `practice/<date>-<mode>-<slug>.md` — the session record

`mode` is `drill` / `mock` / `real` (debrief: `<date>-real-<company>.md`) / `transcript`. Verbatim questions, candidate answers, and scoring breakdowns.

## `composite-target.md` — synthetic drill profile

Synthesized from top `jd-analysis/` files: frequency-weighted competencies, question themes, and interview formats.

## `negotiation/<company>.md` — stated comp record

Comp numbers stated in interview rounds (number, date, recipient). Read by day-of cheat sheets for consistency.
