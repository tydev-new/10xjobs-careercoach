C — Search home + conversation

Owner's pick · every screen builtOpen direction-c-home.html →

Thesis. A job search is a place you come back to, not only a chat you scroll. A home page shows where the search stands — the pipeline counts, this week's short plan, the active application and its stage — and "Continue with Ten" is one tap away. The conversation stays the single place where Ten works and where you say yes.

Structure, in one sentence. A left rail (Home · Talk to Ten · Jobs · Applications · Documents, then the active application, then your account) beside one page at a time; Talk to Ten is the thread with the document pinned on its right; on a phone the rail becomes a five-tab bar with Ten in the middle.

Home, 1440 × 900, light. Every number on it is in the mvp-journey fixture (jobs.md, criteria.md, plan.md), except the $4.20 balance, borrowed from gate-moment — see README.
Talk to Ten with the tailored résumé pinned on the right (target-voice copy).

Why this one needs a doc change. docs/design-web-ui.md § 1 rules out "no other screens in the MVP — no jobs table". The Part 1 reason for the rail is rule 2: "the sign it's working: the numbers change every week" — today the only way to see those numbers is to ask the coach. The architect must amend § 1 before this is built; this task does not edit it.

The brand mark

A rounded tile holding a "1" bar and an oval "0" — Ten read as the numeral, which also ties to 10xjobs. Same geometry in all three; the colour follows the direction (A ink/white, B ink-blue/white, C ink with a lime "0"). A heavier-stroke version is the favicon (faviconHref() in each file) and reads at 16 px. No sparkle, no emoji, no gradient.
