# Cover letter — Structural Engineer, Terrastack Engineering

Dear Terrastack hiring team,

Your posting asks for a "seismic guru" — and I'll admit the phrase made me
smile, because the engineers I most respect would never accept the title.
What I can offer instead is eleven years of lateral-system work with the
calculations to back every claim, which I suspect is what you actually
meant.

At Calder & Moss I've been the design lead on two mid-rise concrete-core
office buildings in downtown Oakland. Both went through external peer
review without a single comment on the lateral analysis, and both closed
out with clean punch lists. Before that I spent five years at Bayline
Transit Authority designing station structures and platform canopies over
live track, where the tolerance for surprises is roughly zero.

The part of your posting that genuinely caught me was the base-isolation
work on the Coleman civic annex. I spent three years in construction
support on the Harbor Bridge seismic retrofit under Dana Whitfield's team,
and the lesson that stuck was that isolation and energy-dissipation systems
live or die on their connections and their maintenance access, not on the
elegance of the analysis. I'd bring that construction-side skepticism to
your design bench.

I keep my tools current — ETABS, Perform-3D, and a healthy distrust of both
— and I hold PE licenses in California and Oregon. My references include
contractors as well as engineers, because I think how a design builds is
part of whether it's good.

I'd welcome a conversation about the annex work or anything else on your
board. Calc packages available on request; job-site stories free of charge.

Thank you for the consideration.

Tomás Rivera, PE
