# A001 — Meridian — No-Show Dashboard, Third Try

Status: confirmed — planted fixture.

## The Short Version (30 seconds)

The first two versions of the no-show dashboard answered questions nobody was asking. I sat with the clinic managers, watched their 7am scheduling huddle, and rebuilt it around the one question they actually ask: who is likely to no-show today and who can we call? Version three got adopted by all 3 managers for daily scheduling decisions.

## What I Learned

Adoption is the metric. Build for the question the user actually asks each morning.
